A small, dimly lit whisky bar tucked into the slopes of Motomachi, popular
with visitors doing a walking tour of the old foreign consulates nearby.
The shelves are stacked with a genuinely impressive range of Japanese
whiskies, and the bartender is happy to talk you through a flight if
you're not sure where to start.

Seating is limited, so it's worth swinging by early evening rather than
turning up at peak hours hoping for a seat.
