An observation tower built beside Goryokaku Park, giving a full aerial
view of Japan's first Western-style star fort, constructed in the final
years of the Edo period. The star shape is genuinely striking from above,
and it's especially popular in spring when the moat is ringed with
cherry blossoms.

Worth pairing with a walk through the park itself afterward — the tower
gives you the shape of the place, the ground level gives you the scale.
